### 🐌 **Economy** - 500 lamports
- **Cost:** ~$0.0001 per trade
- **Speed:** Slow (5-10s confirmation)
- **Best for:** Low volume, stable tokens, saving money
- **Example:** Running 1,000 trades = $0.10 in fees

### ⚖️ **Standard** - 1,500 lamports (Default)
- **Cost:** ~$0.0003 per trade
- **Speed:** Medium (2-5s confirmation)
- **Best for:** Normal trading, balanced approach
- **Example:** Running 1,000 trades = $0.30 in fees

### ⚡ **Fast** - 5,000 lamports
- **Cost:** ~$0.001 per trade

- **Example:** Running 1,000 trades = $1.00 in fees

### 🚀 **Turbo** - 10,000 lamports
- **Cost:** ~$0.002 per trade
- **Speed:** Very Fast (<1s confirmation)
- **Best for:** Sniping, urgent trades, high competition
- **Example:** Running 1,000 trades = $2.00 in fees

### 🎯 **Custom** - You choose!

- **Best for:** Advanced users who know exactly what they need

## 🎮 How to Change Fee Mode

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Open Telegram bot**
2. **Go to Volume Trading** → 📊 Volume Settings
3. **Click ⚡ Fee Settings** (new button)
4. **Select your preferred mode:**
   - Tap 🐌 Economy for slow/cheap
   - Tap ⚖️ Standard for balanced
   - Tap ⚡ Fast for competitive
   - Tap 🚀 Turbo for urgent
   - Tap 🎯 Custom to enter your own
5. **Done!** Your choice is saved

- ✅ All volume trading modes (Safe, Instant, Delayed, FOMO)
- ✅ Both pump.fun and DEX tokens
- ✅ All wallet groups
- ✅ Current and future sessions
